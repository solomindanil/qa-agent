# Outcome-completion contract extension — visible content

This extends [the frozen first-trial cases](cases.md) for the later local
regression controls. The first four actor designs and their original contract
are historical evidence; they were not rerun against this extension.

## OC-G: applicable instructions

The synthetic `/requirements` response also declares `guideDetail`:
`Import the Nebula profile, then connect in Nebula.` On `/guide`, both the
`Nebula setup` heading and this instruction must be rendered by the flat
fixture. A correct heading with old Atlas instructions, or text hidden via
the injected `hidden` or ancestor `opacity:0` variants, fails.
This text is a controlled fixture promise, not a rule for a real VPN provider.

## OC-Q and OC-P: visible results

The selected quantity's rendered total must pass the same controls after its
matching quote response. After Save, `Daily plan` must exist in the independent
state read and pass the controls after reopening `/notes`. The evaluator checks
Playwright visibility, zero opacity on the test-id element or an ancestor, and
exact rendered `innerText` rather than hidden-descendant `textContent`.

These are bounded DOM-rendering controls, **not** a complete human-perception
oracle. Transparent text-bearing descendants, transparent text color, offscreen
placement and occlusion are not independently excluded by this extension.

The existing request/response, independent state, healthy-neighbor and
blocked-scope requirements in the frozen cases still apply. These controls
do not establish the current content of a deployed product or an unaided
agent result.
