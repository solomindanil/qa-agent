## Freeland Balance boundary

Selecting Freeland Balance with a positive amount automatically POSTs `/api/cards/:cardId/freeland-balance-topups/quote`. At c469 this calls `ensureProviderClient`, which may conditionally upsert `provider_clients`. The quote does not create a provider top-up or money hold, but its name and POST body alone do not establish mutation-free behaviour. It is outside the current dry allowlist and is **not** silently admitted in this slice.

The future controlled lane needs an explicit effect classification and appropriately prepared account state. Until then, retain the concrete quote gap, continue independent checks, and never replace it with a local estimate, hidden skip or all-method PASS. The actual create endpoint, checkout submit and all funding actions remain forbidden. No owner action is needed to continue the independent local implementation.
