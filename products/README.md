# Product and campaign ownership

The requested product and its existing owner select the specialist, environment, candidate, frozen runtime and tracker. The [manifest](../sources/manifest.v1.json) selects delivered source only; it cannot migrate a campaign or grant product access. For an unknown product, begin with [qa-check](../skills/qa-check/SKILL.md) and then read the complete selected specialist. If no existing pack is selected, [qa-product-v0](../components/console/skills/qa-product-v0/SKILL.md) can analyze the product before registration; [qa-init](../components/console/skills/qa-init/SKILL.md) is for actual registration or its defined recovery, not every analysis.

| Context | Execution / knowledge owner | Initial action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freeland | Specialized source in `components/freeland`; an existing campaign retains its own frozen harness, graph and campaign owner | Resolve the latest owner `CURRENT` checkpoint and the release skill/runbook before commands. Preserve paid-operation evidence and unknown outcomes; do not switch runtime or repeat an uncertain invoice/payment action. |
| Agentify | Existing registered workspace and runtime selected by its owner checkpoint | Retain managed evidence, historical review and unsealed observations separately. Do not re-register. |
| MagicCard / MagicPay pilot | External registered pack owned by its campaign | Read the [second-product checkpoint](../docs/qualification/second-product.md), then resolve exact paths, pins and authority from its current owner. Do not move or replace its campaign. |
| New or Starter-managed product | Console with the selected Kernel, if that lane is chosen and supported | Ground product requirements, risks and available capabilities before a plan or registration. Keep its own map, graph, rules and data. |
| Nuanu / public-auth fixture | Synthetic Console fixture only | Harness regression material, **not** an accepted live product pack; see its [bounded qualification](../docs/qualification/learning-portability.md). |

Historical task IDs, source hashes and machine-local paths can help locate an existing owner, but are not first-use inputs or current authority. On the original host, `~/.codex/qa-workspaces.md` is only a locator index; a fresh clone must resolve its own product owner and private paths. If owner state cannot be resolved, continuation of that exact campaign is blocked; separately authorized new analysis is not. Never register a replacement workspace as an implicit migration.

Use distinct private product/run paths. The [Starter setup](../docs/getting-started.md#3-configure-console-and-kernel-explicitly-starter-lane-only) explains explicit state/store/registry variables for a new selected Console operation; its examples do not move existing registrations. Do not invent a universal `full` or `ticket` CLI: Starter builds a validated plan from its actual catalog/graph and Freeland has its own routes. A complete review reports known requirements, dependent paths and unknown/unrun scope. A source-only graph does not prove deployment identity; one product's acceptance does not prove another's.
